About Dash 360 Technologies

Mobile vehicle technology.
Professional service.

Dash 360 Technologies provides mobile dash camera and vehicle electronics installation for personal vehicles and commercial fleets across Central Florida.

We bring the installation to the customer, reducing the need to leave a vehicle at a traditional installation shop.

Who We Are

Built around service, installation and accountability.

Dash 360 Technologies was created to provide professional mobile installation for the growing amount of technology being added to personal and commercial vehicles.

Our focus includes dash cameras, hardwire systems, parking-mode integration and expanding mobile fleet-electronics services.

Rather than requiring customers to bring every vehicle to a shop, Dash 360 is structured around mobile service at homes, workplaces and commercial fleet locations.
